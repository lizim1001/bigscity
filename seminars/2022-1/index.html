Review of Seminars in 2022

 

The 1st BIGSCITY Seminar (2022.12)

 

 On December 30, 2022, BIGSCITY Lab held the first spatiotemporal data mining seminar. Four students shared the cutting-edge progress in the field of spatiotemporal data mining. The shared topics included spatiotemporal self-supervised learning, multi-agent reinforcement Learning, trajectory representation learning and trajectory completion methods.
