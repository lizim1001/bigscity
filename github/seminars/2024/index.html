Review of Seminars in 2024

 

The 13th BIGSCITY Seminar (2024.10.27)

 

The 13th BIGSCITY seminar was held both online and offline on October 27, 2024. During the seminar, four students gave presentations: Dr. Jiahao Wen explored the frontier of reinforcement learning in the financial domain, Dr. Ying Pang elaborated on the application of neural ordinary differential equations in traffic prediction, Dr. Yilong Zhou introduced a time series forecasting study from Alibaba DAMO Academy presented at ICLR'24, and Dr. Mingxin Dai summarized research on the intersection of chaos theory and deep learning. The event was successfully concluded. read more

Yilong Zhou:         Time Series Forecasting Based on Channel-Aligned Robust Hybrid Transformer [slides]

 

Mingxin Dai:         Chaos Theory and Deep Learning [slides]

The 12th BIGSCITY Seminar (2024.3.14)

 

The 12th BIGSCITY Seminar was held both online and offline on March 14, 2024. During the seminar, three students gave presentations covering topics such as Vision Transformers and registers, spatiotemporal data extrapolation, and multi-map entity representation learning. The event was successfully concluded. read more
